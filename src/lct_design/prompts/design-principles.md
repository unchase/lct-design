Presentation composition and readability rules.
Treat the uploaded brand design as the visual authority. Preserve its backgrounds, artwork,
panels, logo, type families and spacing relationships. Do not replace it with generic cards.
Make the main message identifiable at a glance through hierarchy and purposeful grouping.
Use space to separate unrelated ideas and proximity to connect related facts. Avoid tiny
captions carrying the entire argument. Keep typography roles consistent and comfortable to read.
Evaluate the whole composition: visual balance, reading order, content density, contrast over
actual artwork, and whether the chosen pattern suits the evidence (comparison, process, data).
Prefer fewer well-used regions over scattering text across decorative shapes. Preserve facts;
when content does not fit, report it or choose another existing pattern, never drop evidence.
Critique only visible evidence, distinguish uncertain judgments from measured failures,
and stop after one bounded repair pass rather than iterating indefinitely.
